    Good to know

    • Planes labeled "scheduled, not live" are drawn from today's timetable, landing or taking off on runway 22 at their scheduled time. Planes labeled "2 min ago" are live from The OpenSky Network, but its receivers lose most flights over the hills.
    • Flight times in the info panel are scheduled times at Imphal, in IST.
    • Buildings, roofs, and trees are drawn from open data, so details may be wrong. Use Suggest a correction at the bottom right to report one.
    • Drag the info panel by its top bar to move it; double-click the bar to put it back. The bar shows the weather in Imphal right now, from Open-Meteo.
    • Water and other animation can be turned off in Settings (⚙).
